About Me

Hi, I'm Joshua Allan P. Pagallaman. I'm currently pursuing my
Bachelor of Science in Computer Science at the Polytechnic University
of the Philippines, Manila, with an expected graduation in July 2027.
I'm a developer who genuinely enjoys the process of building thoughtful
software, whether that means designing human-centric interfaces or
architecting the backend systems that power them.

In my recent roles as a Backend Developer at ByteMinds Software
Solutions and an intern at Oikos Technologies, I focused on writing
clean, modular code and optimizing system performance. I enjoy working
with technologies like Java, Spring Boot, Node.js, and React. I approach
my projects with a sense of kodawari, a quiet dedication to the craft,
ensuring that what I build is not only functional but highly maintainable
and scalable.

Beyond my day-to-day coding, I love collaborating with others to solve
real-world problems. This collaborative drive has led me to pitch and
build solutions at events like the Byteme UST CSSprint, where my team
won first place by focusing on empathetic AI. I'm also deeply committed
to fostering a culture of shared learning as an Executive Director for
the Microsoft Student Community, where I organize workshops to help
upskill my fellow students.
